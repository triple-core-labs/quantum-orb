How an orb is decided

Paying for an orb and learning its result are two separate steps. Your payment records the block it landed in; the result is derived from the hash of a later block, which did not exist when you paid. Nobody, including us, can predict or change it. The rules and the odds are in the contract and are published on our provably fair page, where any result can be recomputed independently.
