Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2024

Quantum Orb runs on a public blockchain. This page describes exactly what we see, what we store and what we cannot delete. It describes what the software actually does today, not what a template says a gambling site usually does.

There is no account

We do not ask for a name, an email address, a date of birth, a password or a payment card. There is no registration and no identity check. You connect a wallet, and that is the whole sign-up.

What we can see

  • Your wallet address, once you connect it. It is already public on the blockchain.
  • What you do with the contract: which orbs you opened, what they paid, who invited you. All of this is written to the blockchain by you, not by us, and anyone can read it.

What we store

Our server keeps an index of those public blockchain events so the leaderboard loads quickly. It holds nothing that is not already on the chain. If our database were deleted tomorrow, it could be rebuilt from the blockchain alone.

What your browser stores

  • One entry in local storage, quantum-orb.referrer, holding the address of whoever invited you. It exists so your referrer can be credited on your first orb. You can clear it in your browser at any time.
  • No cookies. No analytics. No advertising or tracking scripts.

What we cannot do

Blockchain records are permanent and public. We cannot edit, hide or delete your transactions, your points or your address from the chain, and neither can anyone else. If you want to keep an activity separate from your identity, use a separate wallet.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Our server talks to a blockchain node to read events and to submit the second half of your orb opening. Your wallet talks to whichever provider you configured in it.

Server logs

Like any web service, our server may record request metadata such as IP addresses for a short period to keep the service running and to stop abuse. These logs are not used to profile you and are not shared.

Children

Quantum Orb is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If what the software does changes, this page changes with it. The date at the top is the last time that happened.
